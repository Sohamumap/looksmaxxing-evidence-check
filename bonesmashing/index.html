Does bonesmashing work, and is it safe?

No evidenceHigh risk

Do not do this.

What the evidence says

There is no evidence that hitting your face makes bone grow in a better shape. Bone does not remodel that way.

What can go wrong

Fractures, nerve damage, lasting swelling and a lopsided face. If you have already done it and have numbness or pain, see a doctor.

What would change this grade

A controlled study showing safe, lasting bone change from repeated impact. None exists, and one could not be run ethically.

Where to start reading

Grillo et al., Journal of Stomatology, Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, 2024. Urgent concern regarding 'bone smashing', a dangerous trend on TikTok.

Its limit: A published warning in a surgery journal, not a study of outcomes.
