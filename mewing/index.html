Does mewing work, and is it safe?

Weak evidenceLow risk

Short answer

Harmless, but no good evidence it reshapes an adult face.

What the evidence says

There are no controlled trials showing that holding your tongue on the roof of your mouth changes adult jaw or cheekbone structure.

What can go wrong

The main cost is time and false hope. Before-and-after photos online mostly show weight loss, age, lighting and camera angle.

What would change this grade

One controlled trial in adults with before-and-after scans. None exists yet.

Sources

No single good study to point to. That absence is part of the grade.
