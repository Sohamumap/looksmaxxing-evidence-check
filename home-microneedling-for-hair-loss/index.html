Does home microneedling for hair loss work, and is it safe?

Mixed evidenceMedium risk

Short answer

Promising, not settled. Clinic-grade results do not transfer automatically to a roller at home.

What the evidence says

A few small trials found microneedling plus minoxidil beat minoxidil alone. The studies are small and the methods vary.

What can go wrong

Infection and scarring if the tool is not sterile or you go too deep or too often.

What would change this grade

Larger trials using home rollers, not clinic devices, that also report infection rates.

Sources

Start with this study: Dhurat et al., International Journal of Trichology, 2013. A randomized evaluator blinded study of effect of microneedling in androgenetic alopecia: a pilot study. Its limit: A pilot study at one clinic, done by clinicians rather than at home.
