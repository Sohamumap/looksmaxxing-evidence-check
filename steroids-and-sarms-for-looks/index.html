Does steroids and sarms for looks work, and is it safe?

Strong evidenceHigh risk

Short answer

They build muscle. The price can be your heart, hormones and fertility.

What the evidence says

The muscle-building effect is real. So is the damage, which is why this is graded on risk, not on whether it works.

What can go wrong

Heart disease, shutdown of your own testosterone, infertility, mood changes. SARMs are unapproved drugs, and products sold online often contain something other than what is on the label.

What would change this grade

Nothing on the harm. The open question is only how often it happens, and at what dose.

Sources

Start with this study: Baggish et al., Circulation, 2017. Cardiovascular toxicity of illicit anabolic-androgenic steroid use. Its limit: An observational study of long-term users, so it shows a link, not proof of cause.
